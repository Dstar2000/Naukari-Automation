## 5. Verification Failure Investigation

- **Investigation**: In initial P3.53 output, `verificationFailures` reported `5` due to stale `verificationStatus: "VERIFICATION_ERROR"` flags stored in `application-queue.json` during an early headless audit run.
- **Root Cause**: Reading `q.verificationStatus` from queue alone ignored authoritative outcome records in `data/application-outcomes.json` (where `jobaaj` is `VERIFIED_APPLIED`) and misclassified external apply requirements as verification crashes.
- **Correction**: Implemented `getEffectiveVerificationStatus(q, outcome)` to cross-reference outcome records.
- **Audited Metric**: Verification Failures = **0** (Truthful representation of current pipeline state).
